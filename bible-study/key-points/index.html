Coach Kenny · Bible Study · Key points

What God started in Genesis, Jesus brings back, and Revelation finishes.

Genesis 1 and 2 is the first page of the Bible. Revelation is the last page. Read them side by side. The same things come back: light, water, the Word, and rest.

Seven things that come back

Read each row left to righttop to bottom. Where it starts in Genesis. How it comes back. How Revelation finishes it.

Jesus brings it backJohn 1:4-9

God finishes itRevelation 21:23

Text says what the verse itself says. One way to understand this. Weigh it yourself. Just a theory an idea offered as a guess, not a teaching.

1Light

Genesis Genesis 1:3 → Jesus John 1:4-9 → Revelation Revelation 21:23

One way

Light came before the sun, and the light is God

In simple terms: The light of day one comes before the sun, and it comes from God. Genesis 1:3–5 John 1:4–9 The end of the Bible matches. The city has no need of the sun, because God gives the light. Revelation 21:23 Revelation 22:5

Genesis 1:3-5, John 1:4-9, Revelation 21:23, 22:5

The sun does not show up until day four. So the light of day one is not sunlight. John 1 says the life was the light of men. At the end there is no need of the sun. God is the light.

One way

God never calls the darkness good, or says He created it

In simple terms: God called the light good. He never says He created the darkness, and He never calls it good. Genesis 1:2–4 At the end there is no night. Revelation 22:5

Genesis 1:2-5

Light is the first thing God makes, and He calls it good. The darkness is already there. God separates it out. That fits the way the Bible ends, with no night at all.

One way

The day 4 lights are different from the day 1 light

In simple terms: God divided light from darkness on day one. Genesis 1:4 The sun, moon, and stars take that job on day four. Genesis 1:14–18 The first light was God. Revelation 21:23

Genesis 1:14-19

A firmament is the wide open space in the sky. On day four God sets lights there to divide the day from the night, for signs and seasons and days and years. Day one already had day and night. So that first light is not the sun. Now the sun, moon, and stars do the job.

Text says

Signs, seasons, and no date-setting

In simple terms: The lights are for signs, seasons, days, and years. Genesis 1:14 People can read the sky and still miss “the signs of the times.” Matthew 16:2–3 It is not for us to know the times or the seasons. Do not set a date. Acts 1:7

Genesis 1:14, Matthew 16:2-3, Acts 1:7

Watch the seasons God gives. Our calendars drift. His timing does not.

“And he said unto them, It is not for you to know the times or the seasons, which the Father hath put in his own power.”Acts 1:7 (KJV)

2Night

Genesis Genesis 1:5 → Jesus John 8:12 → Revelation Revelation 21:25, 22:5

Text says

Day and Night match the end of the Bible

In simple terms: God called the light Day and the darkness Night. Genesis 1:5 The last pages match. “There shall be no night there,” and God gives the light. Revelation 21:25 Revelation 22:5

Genesis 1:5, Revelation 21:25, 22:5

The first page and the last pages use the same words. God is the light. In His city there is no night.

One way

Evening and morning: real days

In simple terms: Each day is “the evening and the morning.” That is a normal day, not a long age. Genesis 1:5 Genesis 1:8 Genesis 1:5

Genesis 1:5, 1:8

The text itself says how God counts a day. That is why the sun and moon are not needed to keep the clock in the first days.

One way

Plants before the sun is no problem for God

In simple terms: Plants came a day before the sun. Genesis 1:11–19 God can keep a plant alive by His own power. Genesis 1:12

Genesis 1:11-19

Plants are made on day three. The sun is made on day four. God was not stuck with the order people expect. He made the plants, and He held them.

3Water

Genesis Genesis 1:6-10, 1:20 → Jesus John 3:5, 7:38 → Revelation Revelation 4:6, 22:1

One way

The firmament: a vault of space

In simple terms: God puts a firmament in the middle of the waters and calls it Heaven. Genesis 1:6–8 A firmament is something beaten out, like a thin sheet stretched wide. It is mostly open space. The sun, moon, and stars are set in it. Genesis 1:14–17 Genesis 1:8

Genesis 1:6-8, 1:14-17

Waters sit above it, and waters sit below it. A vault is mostly empty room under a thin firm roof. That one picture makes the word easier to see.

One way

The waters above are the sea of glass

In simple terms: One way to understand this: the waters above the firmament are the sea of glass at the floor of heaven. Genesis 1:7 Before the throne there is “a sea of glass like unto crystal.” Revelation 4:6 Revelation 4:6

Genesis 1:7-10, Revelation 4:6

The waters below became the seas. Revelation 4:6 is the match for the waters above.

One way

More than one heaven

In simple terms: Verse 1 says God created “the heaven.” Genesis 1:1 Verse 8 calls the firmament Heaven. A firmament is the wide open space. Genesis 1:8 The verse says a man was “caught up to the third heaven.” 2 Corinthians 12:2 The sun, moon, and stars are set in that space. Genesis 1:17

Genesis 1:1, 1:8, 2 Corinthians 12:2

Here are three heavens. The first is the sky where birds fly. The verse says “fowl that may fly above the earth in the open firmament of heaven.” Genesis 1:20 The second is outer space, inside the firmament. The third is where God’s throne is. The verse says a man was “caught up to the third heaven.” 2 Corinthians 12

Text says

The waters gather so the land can appear

In simple terms: The dry land was under the waters. It appeared when they gathered. Genesis 1:9–10 Those waters became today’s seas. Genesis 1:10

Genesis 1:9-10

God says, let the waters gather “unto one place,” and “let the dry land appear.” The land was already there, under the water. Then it comes up.

One way

Life from the water, and born of water

In simple terms: One way to understand this: life from the water pictures the first birth, and the verse adds a second birth, of the Spirit. Genesis 1:20 John 3:3–6 “Born of water” is not a bath. A person must be born again. John 3:6

Genesis 1:20-23, John 3:3-6

On day five the waters bring forth creatures that have life. The verse says a man must be born of water and of the Spirit. John 3 One way to understand this: born of water is the birth of a baby. Born of the Spirit is the new birth.

One way

Abundant water, rivers of living water

In simple terms: The waters bring forth life “abundantly.” Genesis 1:20 One way to understand this: that pictures “rivers of living water,” which is the Spirit. John 7:38–39 John 7:39

Genesis 1:20, John 7:37-39

John 7:38 says rivers of living water will flow from the believer. The next verse says that is the Holy Spirit. One way to understand this: God was already thinking of what the Spirit would do.

One way

Two meanings of "earth"

In simple terms: In verse 1 the earth is the whole world. Genesis 1:1 In verse 10, Earth is the dry land. Genesis 1:10 One verse says God “hangeth the earth upon nothing.” Job 26:7 Another says “the pillars of the earth are the LORD’s.” 1 Samuel 2:8 Ask which earth, and the verses fit. Genesis 1:10

Genesis 1:1, 1:10, Job 26:7, 1 Samuel 2:8

The same first page uses both meanings. They do not fight.

“He stretcheth out the north over the empty place, and hangeth the earth upon nothing.”Job 26:7 (KJV)

Just a theory

Four lifeless days, then life (a theory)

In simple terms: This is only a theory. The verse says one day is with the Lord as a thousand years. 2 Peter 3:8 Four days with no creature, then life, may picture the years before the Son came “when the fulness of the time was come.” Galatians 4:4 Everlasting life is through Him. John 3:16

2 Peter 3:8, Galatians 4:4, John 3:16

The creation days are still real days, marked evening and morning. This picture is a guess, not a teaching.

“…one day is with the Lord as a thousand years, and a thousand years as one day.”2 Peter 3:8 (KJV)

4The Word

Genesis Genesis 1:3 → Jesus John 1:1-3 → Revelation Revelation 19:13

One way

The first thing the Bible says is God

In simple terms: “In the beginning God.” God is set right next to the beginning. Genesis 1:1

Genesis 1:1

The first word after “In the beginning” is “God.” Nothing comes before Him. That is how the whole Bible opens.

One way

The Word that spoke creation is the Word of John 1

In simple terms: God said, “Let there be light,” and there was light. Genesis 1:3 The Word made all things. John 1:1–3 The verse says the heavens were made “by the breath of his mouth.” Psalm 33:6

Genesis 1:3, John 1:1-3

John 1:1-3 says who the Word is. All things were made by Him. So when God speaks in Genesis 1, the Word is at work. Creation is made by speaking.

One way

"Kind" is God's plain word

In simple terms: “Kind” is God’s plain word. Genesis 1:11–25 Stay with His wording. Do not trade it for “science falsely so called.” 1 Timothy 6:20

Genesis 1:11-25

A child can tell a horse from a cat. Nobody needs a lab book to follow the story. Keep God’s word as He wrote it.

Text says

God numbers and names every star

In simple terms: “He made the stars also.” Genesis 1:16 God “telleth the number of the stars; he calleth them all by their names.” Psalm 147:4 His understanding is infinite. Psalm 147:5

Genesis 1:16, Psalm 147:4

Psalm 147:4 says God counts the stars and calls every one by name. He already knows each star. He is far greater than people who struggle to count them.

Just a theory

Adam named the animals in under a day (a theory)

In simple terms: This is only a theory. On one day: the animals, then Adam, then he names them, then the woman is made. Genesis 2:19–23 That would show the mind God gave mankind. Genesis 2:19

Genesis 1:27, 2:19-23

It all fits inside day six, if this theory is right. It is a guess, not a fact. The day is still a normal day.

Text says

Great whales, and Jonah

In simple terms: Genesis names “great whales.” Genesis 1:21 The story says “a great fish.” Jonah 1:17 Another verse says “in the whale’s belly.” Matthew 12:40 Leave God’s words as they stand. Psalm 12:6

Genesis 1:21, Jonah 1:17, Matthew 12:40

God names His creatures in plain words. The verse made that story the sign of death and rising again.

“For as Jonas was three days and three nights in the whale’s belly; so shall the Son of man be three days and three nights in the heart of the earth.”Matthew 12:40 (KJV)

5"Us"

Genesis Genesis 1:26 → Jesus Matthew 28:19 → Revelation Revelation 22:1, 22:17

One way

The Spirit moves, then God speaks

In simple terms: The Spirit of God moved on the waters. Then God spoke. Genesis 1:2–3 Spirit also means breath, the life that returns to God. Ecclesiastes 12:7

Genesis 1:2-3

The Spirit moves first. Then the voice of God. Then creation answers.

One way

"Let us make man": the Trinity on page one

In simple terms: God says, “Let us make man in our image,” and then man is made “in his own image.” One God, more than one Person. Genesis 1:26–27 It cannot be angels. Only God creates. Genesis 1:27

Genesis 1:26-27

Triune means three in one. This is a first look at that, in the first chapter. Angels do not make anyone.

One way

Man rules the earth, not the heavens

In simple terms: Dominion means rule. It is over the earth, not over the heavens. Genesis 1:26 A tower was planned whose top may reach unto heaven. Genesis 11:3–4 Another verse says, “I will ascend into heaven.” Isaiah 14:12–14

Genesis 1:26, Genesis 11:3-4, Isaiah 14:12-14

One way to understand this: man’s job is the earth. The heavens stay God’s. Isaiah 14 says, “I will ascend into heaven.”

One way

Lucifer ruled the earth before man

In simple terms: One way to understand this: the one who wanted to ascend ruled under the stars, had been in Eden, and fell before the six days. Isaiah 14:12–14 Ezekiel 28:13–15 That is why the serpent is already there, and hostile. Genesis 3:1

Genesis 1:28, Isaiah 14:12-14, Ezekiel 28:13-15, Genesis 3:1

The six days are “very good,” so the fall does not fit inside them. The serpent is already in the garden in Genesis 3. One way to understand this: the fall comes before the six days.

“…I will ascend into heaven, I will exalt my throne above the stars of God…”Isaiah 14:13 (KJV)

6After his kind: one Designer

Genesis Genesis 1:11-25 → Jesus Colossians 1:16 → Revelation Revelation 4:11

One way

Five building blocks in one verse

In simple terms: One short verse holds time (“beginning”), the act of making (“created”), space (“heaven”), and matter (“earth”). Genesis 1:1

Genesis 1:1

Use it as a memory picture. A lot is packed into the first line.

One way

One verse answers the big worldviews

In simple terms: Verse 1 says God made the heaven and the earth. Genesis 1:1 The verse says he “formed it to be inhabited.” It is not an accident, and it did not always exist. Isaiah 45:18

Genesis 1:1

One line answers a lot. God is one, not many gods. God is real. The world was made. It did not make itself. It had a start.

Text says

Plants and seed in themselves

In simple terms: Each plant has “seed in itself” and grows up after its kind. Genesis 1:11–12

Genesis 1:11-13

God says, let the earth bring forth grass, herbs, and fruit trees. The seed is built in from the start. Gardeners still use that seed to grow the same fruit.

One way

"After his kind" is the key phrase

In simple terms: “After his kind” is said again and again. A living thing does not become a different kind. Genesis 1:11–12 Genesis 1:21 Genesis 1:24–25 Genesis 1:21

Genesis 1:11-12, 21, 24-25

Plants make plants. Birds make birds. Cattle make cattle. A dog can have different kinds of dogs. A dog does not have a cat. God says it so many times that it is hard to miss.

One way

A common designer, not a common ancestor

In simple terms: Living things can look alike because one Designer made them, not because one kind gave birth to another. Genesis 1:21–25 The things that are made show His work, so people are “without excuse.” Romans 1:20

Genesis 1:11-12, 21-25

Artists leave their style in their work. The same style does not mean one painting gave birth to the next.

Text says

Insects and animals made the same day

In simple terms: Insects, cattle, and beasts were made on the same day. Genesis 1:24–25 They are not separated by long ages. Genesis 1:25

Genesis 1:24-25

On day six the earth brings forth cattle, creeping things, and beasts, each “after his kind.” Bugs, farm animals, and wild animals show up together.

One way

Be fruitful: plants, animals and man share the same words

In simple terms: God uses the same words for plants, animals, and people: seed, fruit, and “be fruitful.” Genesis 1:11 Genesis 1:22 Genesis 1:28 That is one Designer, using one pattern. Genesis 1:28

Genesis 1:11, 22, 28

Plants yield seed and fruit. Fish and birds are told to be fruitful and multiply. Then people hear the same blessing. The link is the Maker, not one creature giving birth to a different kind.

One way

Male and female had to fit from the start

In simple terms: “Male and female created he them.” The two have to fit from the start. Genesis 1:27 That points to a mind behind creation. Genesis 1:27

Genesis 1:27

One way to understand this: life often comes in two kinds that fit together. Both parts have to work on day one. That looks like a mind put it together on purpose.

One way

Fill, then replenish

In simple terms: Fish and birds fill the waters. Genesis 1:22 Adam is told to replenish the earth. Genesis 1:28 So is Noah, after the flood. Genesis 9:1 Israel filled Egypt the first time. Exodus 1:7 The word choice points to a world before Adam. Genesis 1:28

Genesis 1:22, 1:28, 9:1, Exodus 1:7

Replenish has two senses in the old dictionary. One is “To fill; to stock with numbers or abundance.” The other is “To recover former fullness.” One way to understand this: replenish can mean to fill again. That is a hint of an earlier world.

“And God blessed Noah and his sons, and said unto them, Be fruitful, and multiply, and replenish the earth.”Genesis 9:1 (KJV)

7Rest and peace

Genesis Genesis 2:1-3, 1:29-30 → Jesus Matthew 11:28 → Revelation Revelation 21:4, Isaiah 11:6-9

Text says

The first diet was plants, for everyone

In simple terms: At the start, people and animals were given plants for food. Genesis 1:29–30 A later verse says the wolf will dwell with the lamb, and the lion will eat straw. That peace comes back. Isaiah 11:6–7

Genesis 1:29-30, Isaiah 11:6-7

In Genesis 1:29-30, “meat” means food, and the food is herbs and fruit. Every beast and bird gets “every green herb.” Nobody ate anybody. Isaiah 11 says that peace returns.

One way

"Very good", even in a fallen world

In simple terms: Everything God made was “very good.” Genesis 1:31 Even after the fall and the flood, the beauty is still His work. Genesis 13:10

Genesis 1:31

Day six ends with God looking at everything He made. It was very good. Look around. Beautiful places are still here. That is His handiwork.

One way

The seventh day: blessed, holy and a pattern

In simple terms: God blessed the seventh day and sanctified it. Sanctified means set apart as holy. Seven keeps showing up. Genesis 2:1–3 Leviticus 25:8–10 The pattern gives clues to the times. No one can set the date. 1 Thessalonians 5:1–2

Genesis 2:1-3, Leviticus (Jubilee, ch. 25†), 1 Thessalonians 5

God did not have to add a seventh day. He could have started over at day one. He blessed this day and made it holy. The Bible keeps coming back to seven. That is not an accident.

One way

"Rest" means to cease, not to get tired

In simple terms: God “rested from all his work.” Rested means He stopped. He was not tired. Genesis 2:2 He is omnipotent, all-powerful. He made man with a breath. Genesis 2:7

Genesis 2:1-3, Genesis 2:7

In Genesis 2:2, rest means to cease, to stop. In Genesis 2:7, God breathed, and man became a living soul. Resting means the work was finished.

One way

"All the host of them"

In simple terms: One way to understand this: “host” means the stars and also the angels, so the verse may sum up all creation. Genesis 2:1 The morning stars and the sons of God are named together. Job 38:7

Genesis 2:1, Nehemiah 9:6, Job 38:7

Genesis 2:1 may sum up everything from Genesis 1:1, not only the six days. Host means both the stars and the angels.

“…thou hast made heaven, the heaven of heavens, with all their host…”Nehemiah 9:6 (KJV)
