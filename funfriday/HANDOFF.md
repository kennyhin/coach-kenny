---

## What it is

Friday playground reward for students who earned a **homerun** that week (great job all week). Only those kids go out. The rest of the class stays and keeps working.

- **Window:** 1:05–2:20 PM
- **Out:** 10 minutes
- **Transition:** 5 minutes after each session so yards can clear
- **Two grades at once**, playing separately:
  - Younger grade → **Kinder playground** (red pad)
  - Older grade → **big playground** (purple pad)
- Grades do **not** have to be neighbors (K+1 is not required)

## Why these pairs

Built from the 26–27 ES daily schedule workbook (K–5 tabs). Protect **Math, Writing, and Specials** as much as possible. ELA/Math matter most.

**Clean pull times (no Math / Writing / Specials):**

- 2nd: 1:25–1:55 Science
- 3rd: 1:05–1:50 Power Hour
- 5th as a whole grade: 2:00–2:20 (two afternoon tracks; both are off Math/Writing/Specials only after 2:00)
- 4th as a whole grade: **2:10–2:20** only
- K: no clean slot — Math the entire window (12:55–2:20)
- 1st: no clean slot — Specials 1:00–1:50, then Math 45

So:
- **2nd + 3rd** is the only pair where both grades are clean.
- **4th and 5th** have to go late because each grade has two teacher tracks.
- **K and 1st** cannot avoid Math in this window. Because only homerun kids leave, they go last. Teacher keeps Math going with everyone else. **Do not pull 1st during Specials.**

4th/5th afternoon tracks (do not ignore when changing times):

- **4th Pair 1** (Swanson/Johnson, LaSpina, Sanchez): Math until 1:25, Math 45 until 2:10, Science 2:10–2:50
- **4th Pair 2** (Tumanuvao, Hernandez): Reading until 1:50, Power Hour 1:50–2:50
- **5th Pair 1** (Bobadilla, Galvan, Christensen): Reading until 1:20, Writing 1:20–1:50, Power Hour 1:50–2:50
- **5th Pair 2** (Law, Young): Math until 1:15, Math 45 until 2:00, Science 2:00–2:50
