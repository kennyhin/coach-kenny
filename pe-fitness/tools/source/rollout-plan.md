# SLAM Academy Presidential Fitness Test — Rollout Plan

Official scores come from the White House tables in `benchmarks.md` (also `official/` PDFs). Standards are by **age**, not grade, and start at **age 6**. Age 5 has no official target.

## Who tests

**Grades 3–5 only test for awards.** Use each child’s **age on test day** unless the White House later defines age differently (it currently does not). Do not invent a benchmark for any age missing from the official chart.

A student earns **Presidential** (top) or **National** (solid) by hitting **one target in each of three categories** — not every event: (1) curl-ups in 1 minute **or** plank; (2) 1-mile run **or** 20-meter beep test; (3) right-angle push-ups **or** pull-ups. Give Presidential if all three meet Presidential; National if all three meet National but not Presidential. Grades 3–5 who complete the test may receive a **Certificate of Participation** even if they do not earn an award.

**Grades K–2 do not test, do not get scored, and do not receive awards or participation certificates.** During the same practice weeks, K–2 get a short, kid-friendly **preview** of the real events (for example: a short plank hold, a few curl-ups, a short run or beep game, wall or knee push-ups, and a flexed-arm hang). The teacher says a simple line such as: “In 3rd grade you’ll get to try the Presidential Fitness Test!” Keep it fun. No clipboards, no cutoffs, no official times or rep counts used for awards.

## When

The official PDF sets no testing window. HHS said schools would start in **fall 2026**. For SLAM grades 3–5: **3–4 practice weeks**, then a testing window.

- **Preferred:** late January–early February 2027 (example: practice Jan. 5–29; test Feb. 1–12, plus one makeup week).
- **Backup:** spring 2027 (late March–April) if winter weather or the PE calendar gets in the way.

Pick one window schoolwide so 3–5 certificates can go out together. Run the K–2 preview during the practice weeks, not during the 3–5 testing window if that helps the gym schedule.

## Practice (3–4 weeks) — grades 3–5

Teach form first, then short tries of the real event. Rotate so every class tries both options in each category. End practice with a practice-test day, then official testing (one category per PE period, plus a makeup day).

## How to run a big 3–5 class

Use **stations** and **partners** (one works, one counts, then switch). You or an aide run the clock or beep track. Record age, sex, and the one score used in each category. For a large class, test **one category per PE period** (three days + a makeup day). Do cardio in the gym or outdoors.

- **Curl-ups:** Whole group, 60 seconds on your “go.” Partner counts reps.
- **Plank:** Hold a straight-body plank. Partner times until form breaks. Several pairs can go at once.
- **One-mile run:** Use a measured loop and know how many laps equal a mile (prior SLAM PE plans have used 10 laps for a mile — confirm the course is measured). Start a big group together. Call out elapsed time as they finish. Record minutes:seconds. Faster is better (the only “at most” event).
- **20m beep test:** Mark two lines 20 meters apart. Students reach the line before each beep. A partner tallies laps. Stop after two missed lines (a simple school rule — the official PDF does not spell this out).
- **Right-angle push-ups:** Partner counts good reps (arms to about a right angle, then a full push-up). No official cadence is published; keep form consistent.
- **Pull-ups:** Small groups at the bar. Count full reps (chin over the bar, arms straighten at the bottom).

**After 3–5 testing:** Compare scores to `benchmarks.md`. Print the matching official certificates. Send them home and keep a class list. If the official PDF does not answer a form or age question, use one simple school rule, write it down, and apply it the same way for every child.
