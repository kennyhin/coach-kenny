Title: Fitness Preview — Fun Stations (All Mini Events)
Grade band: Grades K-2
When: Week 4

Unit Objective: Students will try kid-friendly mini versions of the Presidential Fitness Test events so they can move safely, have fun, and learn that in 3rd grade they will get to try the official test.

Unit Essential Question: How can I try new fitness skills, stay safe, and have fun while I get a preview of the Presidential Fitness Test?

Lesson Objective: Students will be able to rotate through kid-friendly mini stations that preview the Presidential Fitness Test events, try each station safely, and repeat that in 3rd grade they will get to try the official test.

Equipment: Mats, wall space, cones and a short run/beep path, optional safe bar for a very short hang, station signs with pictures (curl-up, plank, short run, beep game, wall/knee push-up, hang), whistle, water, optional music. No clipboards, no official score sheets, no award charts.

Bell Ringer/Warm-up:
Enter and sit on squad spots. Warm-up (about 4-5 minutes): a "tour jog" with the coach around the stations (walk and point), jumping jacks, and a freeze. Freeze on the attention signal.

Direct Instruction (TW):
In every K-2 lesson, say this line clearly at least once: "In 3rd grade you'll get to try the Presidential Fitness Test!"

Today we visit all the preview stations we have been trying: a few curl-ups, a short plank, a short run or beep game, wall or knee push-ups, and a flexed-arm hang or safe hang. We are still not testing. There are no passing scores. The goal is to try, be safe, and have fun.

Show each station in one sentence and the rotation direction. When the whistle blows, clean up your spot and walk to the next station. Partners or small groups stay together.

Guided Practice (SW):
Five or six short stations, about 4 minutes each, whistle to rotate. Suggested stations:

1. A few curl-ups (slow, comfortable number).
2. Short superhero plank (kneeling allowed).
3. Short run/walk loop.
4. Beep game on a short pair of lines (no elimination).
5. Wall or knee push-ups.
6. Flexed-arm hang or dead hang if the bar is safe; if not, crab walks or "strong arms" animal walks.

Coach roams, cheers, and helps with safe form. If a class is small, fewer stations are fine. If a class is large, double a station so lines stay short.

Near the last rotation, gather the class and say the 3rd-grade line together.

Cool-down:
Walk the station path once, then a group stretch and slow breaths (3 min). Thank them for trying every station.

Kagan/SLAMified — Timed Pair Share:
Round 1: "My favorite station was ___ because ___." Round 2: "In 3rd grade you'll get to try the Presidential Fitness Test!" (practice saying the line). 20-30 seconds each.

Exit Ticket:
Thumbs: "I tried the stations safely." Show-me a freeze on the signal. Coach notes any station that was too crowded so it can be split next time this preview is taught.

Reminders:
K-2 still gets no scores, no official testing, no awards, and no participation certificates. Do not sneak in official timing "just for fun" if it will be compared or sent home as a result.
Keep the tone proud and forward-looking: 3rd grade will be their turn to try the real test.
Safety stays the same as Weeks 1-3, especially at the bar and on the running lines.

Nevada State Standards:
1.2.1 (K-2) — Demonstrate the basic elements of movement forms.
1.2.4 (K-2) — Demonstrate safe practices while participating in physical activities.
2.2.3 (K-2) — Identify appropriate safety practices in general space for self and others.
