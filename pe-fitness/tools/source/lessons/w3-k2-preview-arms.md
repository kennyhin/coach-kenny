Title: Fitness Preview — Wall/Knee Push-ups and Flexed-Arm Hang
Grade band: Grades K-2
When: Week 3

Unit Objective: Students will try kid-friendly mini versions of the Presidential Fitness Test events so they can move safely, have fun, and learn that in 3rd grade they will get to try the official test.

Unit Essential Question: How can I try new fitness skills, stay safe, and have fun while I get a preview of the Presidential Fitness Test?

Lesson Objective: Students will be able to try wall push-ups or knee push-ups and a short flexed-arm hang or dead hang (or skip the hang if no safe bar is available), using safe hands and waiting their turn.

Equipment: Wall space, mats, a low or regular pull-up bar if you have a safe one (or a sturdy playground bar), a waiting cone, whistle, water. No official rep charts. If no bar is available, skip the hang and use wall push-ups plus animal walks only.

Bell Ringer/Warm-up:
Enter and sit on squad spots. Recap: we previewed tummy strength and running games. Today is arms. Warm-up (about 4-5 minutes): arm circles, "spider walks" on the wall, shoulder squeezes, freeze game. Freeze on the attention signal.

Direct Instruction (TW):
In every K-2 lesson, say this line clearly at least once: "In 3rd grade you'll get to try the Presidential Fitness Test!"

In 3rd grade, students may do right-angle push-ups and pull-ups on the official test. We are not testing those today. We will try kid-friendly versions: push-ups on the wall or from the knees, and a short hang with bent or straight arms if the bar is safe.

Model wall push-ups: feet back a little, hands on the wall, bend the arms, push the wall away. Model knee push-ups on a mat. Model a flexed-arm hang: if a student can, chin near the bar and hold for a short, comfortable time — or just hang with straight arms for a second or two and come down with help. This is a try, not a contest.

Safety: one student on the bar at a time. Wait at the cone. Come down with control. No jumping off a high bar. No pulling on other students.

Vocabulary: wall push-up, knee push-up, hang, wait your turn.

Guided Practice (SW):
Two stations, then switch (about 8 minutes each). Partners cheer; they do not keep official scores.

Station 1 — Push-up choices: wall push-ups or knee push-ups. Try a small set (a few reps), rest, try again. Celebrate any honest try.

Station 2 — Hang preview: short flexed-arm hang or dead hang, or a "strong shoulders" shrug hang. If the line is long, waiting students do wall push-ups or crab walks in a marked area. Keep turns very short so everyone gets a try.

Cool-down:
Arm-across-chest stretches and easy shoulder rolls (2-3 min).

Kagan/SLAMified — RallyRobin:
With a partner, take turns naming a way to stay safe at the bar or wall (wait at the cone, one at a time, come down carefully, feet on the floor for wall push-ups). Two rounds.

Exit Ticket:
Show a wall-push-up hand position, or thumbs: "I tried a push-up and I waited my turn." Ask someone to say the 3rd-grade line.

Reminders:
No official pull-up or push-up numbers, no awards, no certificates. Do not compare who hung longer in a way that leaves kids embarrassed.
If the bar feels unsafe for this age, skip it and stay with wall/knee push-ups and animal walks. Safety beats a preview.

Nevada State Standards:
1.2.1 (K-2) — Demonstrate the basic elements of movement forms.
1.2.4 (K-2) — Demonstrate safe practices while participating in physical activities.
2.2.3 (K-2) — Identify appropriate safety practices in general space for self and others.
