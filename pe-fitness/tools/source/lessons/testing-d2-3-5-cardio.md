Title: Presidential Fitness Test — Official Testing Day 2 (Cardio)
Grade band: Grades 3-5
When: Testing Day 2

Unit Objective: Students will demonstrate safe form and honest effort on the six Presidential Fitness Test events and complete official testing in all three categories (core, cardio, and upper body) so they can earn a Presidential Award, a National Award, or a Certificate of Participation.

Unit Essential Question: How do I train, choose one event in each category, and show my best honest effort on the Presidential Fitness Test?

Lesson Objective: Students will be able to complete their official cardio event (one-mile run or 20-meter beep test), with a recorded mile time (min:sec) or beep-test lap count for every student present who is assigned this day.

Equipment: Measured mile course (confirm lap count; prior SLAM PE plans have used 10 laps for a mile), cones, stopwatch, 20-meter lines, beep-test audio or whistle backup, official score sheets, pencils, water, shade if outdoors, whistle.

Bell Ringer/Warm-up:
Meet at the outdoor course or in the gym, depending on which event you can run first. Attendance and choices checked on the score sheet. Dynamic warm-up (about 5-6 minutes): easy jog, high knees, butt kicks, walking lunges or knee lifts, one easy practice lap or two easy 20m down-and-backs. Freeze. Students may change the cardio choice before the official start, then it is locked for this category.

Direct Instruction (TW):
Official cardio today. Pick one: mile or beep test.

Mile: same direction, measured course, start as a group or in waves if the course is crowded. Coach runs the official clock and calls elapsed time as students finish (or records each finisher). Write minutes:seconds. Faster is better. Students must complete the full measured mile; a partial distance is not an official mile score (mark makeup if they cannot finish safely).

Beep test: 20-meter lines, partner or designated counter tallies laps, two-miss school rule, then stop. Write laps. If both events must happen today, run the mile group first while beep-test students wait in a designated area, then set the 20m course and run beep waves. Do not mix the two on the same path at the same time.

Safety briefing: walk or stop if dizzy, overheated, or in pain, and tell the coach. Water after finishing. Stay clear of runners still on the course.

Official cardio targets for common 3-5 ages (Presidential / National). Look up other ages in benchmarks.md.
Boys mile: age 8 = 8:48 / 11:05; 9 = 8:31 / 10:30; 10 = 7:57 / 9:48; 11 = 7:32 / 9:20. Boys beep laps: 8 = 39 / 19; 9 = 47 / 23; 10 = 55 / 26; 11 = 60 / 31.
Girls mile: age 8 = 10:02 / 12:30; 9 = 9:30 / 11:52; 10 = 9:19 / 11:22; 11 = 9:02 / 11:17. Girls beep laps: 8 = 29 / 15; 9 = 35 / 18; 10 = 41 / 21; 11 = 46 / 24.

Guided Practice (SW):
This period is the official cardio test.

1. Start the mile group. A helper or partner can confirm lap counts if you use a multi-lap course. Coach is the official timer.
2. After the mile group is finished and clear, run the beep-test waves. Counters tally; coach oversees misses and final lap numbers.
3. Early finishers cool down and sit in a quiet zone. No official upper-body testing today.

Mark absences and medical stops as makeup. Do not invent a time or lap count.

Cool-down:
Walk a full easy lap (or the long way around the gym). Calf, quad, and hamstring stretches (3-4 min). Water.

Kagan/SLAMified — Mix-Pair-Share:
After everyone is cooled down and scores are written. Walk, pair on the signal. "Name one thing that helped you pace or reach the line." Two quick rounds. Keep it short.

Exit Ticket:
Coach verifies a mile time or a beep lap count (or a makeup mark) for every student present. Thumbs: "My cardio score is written down."

Reminders:
Confirm the course measurement before the first wave. Post the lap count. Use the same two-miss beep rule as practice.
Heat plan: shorten the wait time in the sun, use shade, and postpone to the makeup day if weather makes testing unsafe.
