Title: Fitness Preview — Short Runs and Beep Games
Grade band: Grades K-2
When: Week 2

Unit Objective: Students will try kid-friendly mini versions of the Presidential Fitness Test events so they can move safely, have fun, and learn that in 3rd grade they will get to try the official test.

Unit Essential Question: How can I try new fitness skills, stay safe, and have fun while I get a preview of the Presidential Fitness Test?

Lesson Objective: Students will be able to complete a short run or walk-run and play a simple start-stop "beep" running game (run to a line, wait, run back) while staying in their lane and freezing on the signal.

Equipment: Cones for a short running path and two lines (a kid-friendly distance — much shorter than 20 meters is fine, such as 8-10 meters or even shorter for kindergarten), whistle or a fun sound for "beeps," water. No stopwatches for official times, no lap-count awards.

Bell Ringer/Warm-up:
Enter and sit on squad spots. Recap in one sentence: last time we tried curl-ups and short planks. Warm-up (about 4-5 minutes): march, skip, side steps, and a freeze game. Freeze on the attention signal.

Direct Instruction (TW):
In every K-2 lesson, say this line clearly at least once: "In 3rd grade you'll get to try the Presidential Fitness Test!"

Today is a preview of running and a "beep" game. In 3rd grade, students may run a full mile or a 20-meter beep test. We are not doing that today. We will do a short, fun version: a little run, then a game where a sound tells us when to run to the line.

Model: run to the cone line before the whistle, stop, turn around, wait for the next whistle, run back. No diving, no pushing, no racing through friends. Walking is always allowed.

Vocabulary: line, whistle/beep, wait, turn, short run.

Guided Practice (SW):
Whole group, then a simple partner cheer job (one runs a turn, one claps, then switch) if that helps a big class.

Round 1 — Short run/walk (5-6 min): a short loop or down-and-back path. Students may run, jog, or walk. Cheer everyone who keeps moving. No official time.

Round 2 — Beep preview game (8-10 min): two lines, short distance. Start slow. Whistle, run to the line, wait, whistle, run back. After a few rounds, you may slightly quicken the whistle, but stop while it is still fun. If a student does not reach the line, they just try the next one — no "you're out," no elimination.

Round 3 — If time: "red light / beep light" on the same lines so kindergarten students can succeed with more waiting time.

Cool-down:
Walk the path once, stretch, slow breaths (3 min). Water.

Kagan/SLAMified — Mix-Pair-Share:
Walk, pair on the signal. Round 1: "Show how you turn at the line." Round 2: "In 3rd grade you'll get to try what test?" Two rounds.

Exit Ticket:
Thumbs: "I ran or walked, and I waited for the sound before I went." Coach notes students who leave early or push at the line for a gentle 1:1 reminder.

Reminders:
This is a preview, not the official 20m beep test or the mile. Do not count official laps or call out times for awards. No certificates.
Short distances for little legs. Lots of walking options. Stop if a student is too tired or too hot.
Say the 3rd-grade line again at dismissal if anyone missed it.

Nevada State Standards:
1.2.1 (K-2) — Demonstrate the basic elements of movement forms.
1.2.4 (K-2) — Demonstrate safe practices while participating in physical activities.
2.2.3 (K-2) — Identify appropriate safety practices in general space for self and others.
