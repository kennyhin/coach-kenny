Title: Presidential Fitness Test — Practice Test Day
Grade band: Grades 3-5
When: Week 4 Day 2

Unit Objective: Students will demonstrate safe form and honest effort on the six Presidential Fitness Test events and complete official testing in all three categories (core, cardio, and upper body) so they can earn a Presidential Award, a National Award, or a Certificate of Participation.

Unit Essential Question: How do I train, choose one event in each category, and show my best honest effort on the Presidential Fitness Test?

Lesson Objective: Students will be able to complete a practice trial of their chosen event in each of the three categories, record practice scores (age, sex, event, score), and confirm or change their official-test choices, with a practice sheet collected for at least 90% of the class.

Equipment: All event equipment (mats, timer, measured 20m lines, beep audio or whistle, measured mile course if practicing the mile today, pull-up bar), practice score sheets, pencils, official target chart (coach and a posted copy), water, whistle, cones.

Bell Ringer/Warm-up:
Enter and sit on squad spots with a practice sheet and a pencil. Dynamic warm-up (about 5 minutes): full-body, including a short jog, 10-second plank, easy curl-ups, and arm circles. Freeze. "Today is a practice test. It feels like the real test, but it does not count for the award. Use it to choose wisely."

Direct Instruction (TW):
How to fill the sheet: first and last name, class, age today, boy/girl as listed on the official tables, the event chosen in each category, and the practice score with units (reps, seconds, min:sec, or laps).

If the class is large, run the practice test like official week: stations by choice — students go to the station for the event they plan to use. Do not require all six events today.

Review school rules quickly: 1-minute curl-ups on the coach's "Go/Stop"; plank timed until form breaks; mile on the measured course, time called at the finish; beep test 20m, two-miss stop, count laps; right-angle push-ups, good reps only; pull-ups, chin over and arms straight at the bottom, one at a time.

A student earns an award by hitting one target in each of the three categories, not every event. The mile is the only "at most" (faster) score; all other events are "at least."

Guided Practice (SW):
Option A (large class, limited time): practice core first (students split to curl-up or plank rows), then a short upper-body rotation at push-up rows and the bar. If you cannot run a full mile today, do a beep practice wave indoors and remind mile choosers of their last practice time from Week 2; if you are already outdoors, run a full practice mile for those who chose it and a beep wave for the others.

Option B (more time): three rotations — core, cardio, upper body — with partners scoring and the coach running the clock or beeps.

Record every practice score on the sheet. Students may change their choice after they see the practice number. Collect sheets at the end so you can plan official testing groups (how many will run the mile vs. the beep test, how many need the bar).

Cool-down:
Easy walk and stretches for the muscles just used (3 min). Water.

Kagan/SLAMified — Timed Pair Share:
Round 1: "My practice score taught me ___." Round 2: "For the official test I will pick ___ in each category because ___." 30 seconds each.

Exit Ticket:
Turn in the practice sheet at line-up. Thumbs: "I know which event I will use in each category on official testing days." Coach flags missing ages, blank scores, or students who were absent for a category — first up on Testing Day 1 or makeup day.

Reminders:
This is not the official test. Do not print award certificates from today's numbers.
Use age on test day later — if a birthday falls before official testing, the official age may change; look up the new age in benchmarks.md.
Confirm the mile course measurement and 20-meter marks before Testing Day 2.
Safety same as practice weeks. Honesty: partners count fair reps. Coach spot-checks.
