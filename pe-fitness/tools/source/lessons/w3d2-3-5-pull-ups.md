Title: Presidential Fitness Test — Pull-ups
Grade band: Grades 3-5
When: Week 3 Day 2

Unit Objective: Students will demonstrate safe form and honest effort on the six Presidential Fitness Test events and complete official testing in all three categories (core, cardio, and upper body) so they can earn a Presidential Award, a National Award, or a Certificate of Participation.

Unit Essential Question: How do I train, choose one event in each category, and show my best honest effort on the Presidential Fitness Test?

Lesson Objective: Students will be able to perform (or safely attempt) pull-ups at the bar with a partner or the coach counting full reps — chin over the bar, arms straight at the bottom — and name pull-ups as the other upper-body choice besides right-angle push-ups.

Equipment: Pull-up bar (or a secure playground bar), gym mats under the bar if needed, step stool only for safe reach if the bar is high, whistle, water, optional practice sheets, a second station of practice push-ups or flexed-arm hangs so the line stays short.

Bell Ringer/Warm-up:
Enter and sit on squad spots. Recap: "Yesterday was right-angle push-ups. Today is pull-ups — the other upper-body choice." Dynamic warm-up (about 5 minutes): arm circles, scapular squeezes, easy dead-hang practice if the bar is free, and 5 knee or regular push-ups. Freeze on the attention signal.

Direct Instruction (TW):
School form rule (the official PDF does not spell out every detail): start from a straight-arm hang, hands on the bar. Pull until the chin is clearly over the bar. Lower until the arms are straight at the bottom. That is one rep. No kipping, no swinging. Partner or coach counts full reps only.

Safety first: only one student on a bar at a time. The next student waits behind a cone. Spot by standing nearby — do not yank a student by the waist unless you are trained and the student has asked for a steadying spot. Feet clear of people walking under the bar. Come down with control; do not drop from a high bar onto hard ground.

Students who cannot yet complete a pull-up still practice: a flexed-arm hang (chin at or above the bar, hold as long as comfortable) or a controlled dead hang and a strong scapular shrug. Those practice moves are for building skill. Official pull-up scores later count full reps only — do not invent a flexed-arm-hang award number; there is no official hang target in the current tables.

Vocabulary: pull-up, hang, chin over the bar, full rep, flexed-arm hang (practice only).

Official right-angle push-ups (reps, at least) and pull-ups (reps, at least) for common 3-5 ages. Look up other ages in benchmarks.md. Age 5 has no official number.
Boys — Push-ups Presidential / National: age 8 = 17 / 9; 9 = 18 / 12; 10 = 22 / 14; 11 = 27 / 15.
Boys — Pull-ups Presidential / National: age 8 = 5 / 1; 9 = 5 / 2; 10 = 6 / 2; 11 = 6 / 2.
Girls — Push-ups Presidential / National: age 8 = 17 / 9; 9 = 18 / 12; 10 = 20 / 13; 11 = 20 / 13.
Girls — Pull-ups Presidential / National: age 8 = 2 / 1; 9 = 2 / 1; 10 = 3 / 1; 11 = 3 / 1.

Guided Practice (SW):
Small groups at the bar, plus a waiting station.

Station A — Bar (rotate): each student gets at least two turns. Partner or coach counts. Record a practice number if you wish. Keep the line moving (about 8-12 min of rotation).

Station B — Waiting work: partner right-angle push-up review or a short plank so no one stands still. When the coach calls "rotate," the next small group comes to the bar.

If you have two bars, split the class and use the same rules at both.

Cool-down:
Walk, then forearm and shoulder stretches (no extra max-effort hangs) (2-3 min).

Kagan/SLAMified — Stand Up, Hand Up, Pair Up:
Round 1: "Tell your partner what counts as a full pull-up." Round 2: "Which two events can you pick for upper body?" Two rounds.

Exit Ticket:
One-word at line-up: "What must go over the bar?" (chin). Coach notes unsafe drops or swinging for a 1:1 safety talk next class.

Reminders:
Pull-up targets can be low and still official — a 10-year-old girl's National target is 1; Presidential is 3. A 10-year-old boy's National target is 2; Presidential is 6. Look up other ages in benchmarks.md. Zero on a practice day is information, not shame.
Do not invent official scores for flexed-arm hangs.
Mats under the bar if the floor is hard. Students with an injury or a medical note sit out the bar and use the alternative station.
