How awards work

Earn the award

“Hit at least one target from every category and you’ll earn the Presidential or National Physical Fitness Award.” All students who complete the test may receive a Certificate of Participation.

Targets go by age on test day, not grade.

Top tier

Presidential

Meet the Presidential target in all three categories.

Solid achievement

National

Meet the National target in all three, but not all Presidential.

Grades 3–5

Participation

For finishing the test. No benchmark is required.

Rollout plan

Practice first, then test

The official PDF does not set a testing window. This is SLAM Academy’s example calendar. Run the K–2 preview during the practice weeks.

  1. 1

    Practice

    Example: Jan 5–29, 2027

    Three to four weeks. Teach form, then short tries of each event. K–2 preview happens here.

  2. 2

    Official test

    Example: Feb 1–12, 2027

    Grades 3–5 only. One category per PE period.

  3. 3

    Makeup

    The following week

    Only for missed or unfairly interrupted official attempts.

  4. 4

    Certificates

    After scores are checked

    Print the matching official certificates and send them home.

Backup window: late March–April 2027, if weather or the PE calendar requires it. Full rollout plan →
