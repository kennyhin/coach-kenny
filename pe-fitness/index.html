Presidential Fitness Test

A national fitness challenge from the White House. This winter, 3rd–5th graders practice and take the test in PE, and K–2 students get a fun preview. Here is what we learn in PE class.

Why we do it

Building healthy habits

The Presidential Fitness Test is a national program that helps students see how strong and fit they are, set goals and feel proud of their progress. It is about effort and growth, not comparing yourself to others.

Grades 3–5 · Take the test

3rd–5th grade

About four weeks of practice in PE, then the official test over three PE classes. Students can earn the Presidential Award, the National Award or a Certificate of Participation.

Grades K–2 · Just for fun

Kindergarten–2nd grade

Fun, kid-sized versions of the events during the same weeks. No testing, no scores and no awards.

What the awards mean

Earning an award

Students who hit the target in all three categories earn an official award from the President’s Council on Sports, Fitness & Nutrition. Every 3rd–5th grader who finishes the test can receive a Certificate of Participation.

Targets go by age on test day, not grade.

Top tier

Presidential

Meets the Presidential target in all three categories.

Solid achievement

Meets the National target in all three categories.

At home

Practice at home

  • Wear sneakers and comfortable clothes, and bring a water bottle on PE days.
  • Get a good night’s sleep and eat breakfast, especially on test days.
  • Keep moving: walks, playground time, or a quick plank challenge.
  • Pick your events, and remember that effort matters more than numbers.
