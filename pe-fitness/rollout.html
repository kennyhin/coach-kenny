Who takes part

Who takes part

Grades 3–5

Grades 3–5 take the test

Students pick one event in each of three categories: core, cardio and upper body. Targets are based on age on test day, not grade.

Hitting the target in all three categories earns the Presidential Award (top tier) or the National Award. Every student who finishes the test can get a Certificate of Participation.

Grades K–2 · Just for fun

K–2 get a preview

During the same weeks, K–2 students try short, kid-sized versions of the events: a few curl-ups, a short plank, short runs and wall push-ups.

There is no testing, no scoring and no awards for K–2. It is all about having fun and getting excited for 3rd grade.

“In 3rd grade you’ll get to try the Presidential Fitness Test!”

When

When it happens

The test happens during regular PE classes this winter. Dates are planned and can shift with the weather or the school calendar.

  1. Planned: Jan. 5–29, 2027

    Practice weeks

    Grades 3–5 learn each event and practice it in PE, about three to four weeks. Grades K–2 do their fun preview during these same weeks.

  2. End of January

    Practice test day

    A dress rehearsal so test day feels familiar. Practice results do not count.

  3. Planned: Feb. 1–12, 2027

    Official test days (grades 3–5)

    One category per PE class: core one day, cardio another, upper body another.

  4. The week after

    Makeup day

    For anyone who was absent or had an attempt interrupted.

  5. After testing

    Certificates are handed out

    Award and participation certificates are handed out to grades 3–5, all at the same time.

  6. Backup

    Spring 2027

    If winter weather or the school calendar gets in the way, testing moves to late March or April 2027.

The six events

What each event looks like

Students choose one event from each pair. Classmates take turns as partners, cheering each other on and helping count.

Core

Curl-ups

As many good curl-ups as possible in one minute, with a partner counting.

Plank

Hold a straight-body plank on the forearms for as long as possible.

Cardio

One-mile run

Run one mile on a measured course. This is the only event where a lower number (a faster time) is better.

20-meter beep test

Run between two lines 20 meters apart, reaching the line before each beep. The beeps slowly speed up.

Upper body

Right-angle push-ups

Body straight, lower until the elbows make about a right angle, then push back up. Only good-form reps count.

Pull-ups

Hang from a bar and pull the chin over it, then lower all the way down.

At home

Practice at home

  • Wear sneakers and comfortable clothes on PE days, and bring a water bottle.
  • A good night’s sleep and breakfast make a real difference, especially on test days.
  • Keep moving: walks, playground time, or a quick plank challenge with a friend.
  • Effort matters more than numbers. Trying your best is what we celebrate.

After testing

Coach Kenny checks each student’s results against the official targets for their age. Then certificates for grades 3–5 are handed out together. Results stay private: no student names or scores are ever posted on this site.
