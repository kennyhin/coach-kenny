Grades 3–5

Right-angle push-ups

Week 3 · Class 1

Students learn right-angle push-ups, one of the two upper-body choices.

On this page
  • In class
  • Why it matters
  • Practice at home

What we do in class

  1. Warm-up: Arm circles, shoulder taps and other easy upper-body moves.
  2. Learning the push-up: Body straight like a board, lower down until the elbows make about a right angle (an “L”), then push all the way back up.
  3. Partner practice: A partner counts only good-form reps, then they switch. Students who need it can build strength with knee or wall push-ups first.
  4. Cool-down: Shoulder and arm stretches.

Why it matters

Push-ups build strength in the arms, chest and shoulders, and good form keeps you safe as you get stronger.

Practice at home

  • Do a few wall or knee push-ups, slow and steady.
  • Can you show what a “right angle” elbow looks like?
