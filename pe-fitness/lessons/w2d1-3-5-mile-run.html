Grades 3–5

One-mile run

Week 2 · Class 1

Students learn how to pace a one-mile run so they can finish strong.

On this page
  • In class
  • Why it matters
  • Practice at home

What we do in class

  1. Warm-up: Easy jogging and dynamic stretches.
  2. The talk test: Students learn a steady pace: if you can still say a short sentence while running, you are not going out too fast.
  3. Practice run: Students run or jog laps on a measured course toward one mile, staying on the path and finishing with control. Walking breaks are fine while they build up.
  4. Cool-down: Walking, water and stretching.

Why it matters

Running builds heart and lung fitness, and learning to pace yourself helps in sports and in life.

Practice at home

  • Go for a walk or jog and practice a pace where you can still chat.
  • Running shoes and a water bottle make a big difference on PE days.
