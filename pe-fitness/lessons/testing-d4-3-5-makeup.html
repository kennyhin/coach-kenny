Official Makeup Day

When: Testing Day 4 Grades 3-5

Equipment

  • pencils

Today we will

Lesson Objective

Students who missed a category or could not complete a fair official attempt will finish the missing official event(s), with the score sheet updated so every 3-5 student who is able has three category scores — or a documented reason they still cannot be scored.

Unit Objective

Students will demonstrate safe form and honest effort on the six Presidential Fitness Test events and complete official testing in all three categories (core, cardio, and upper body) so they can earn a Presidential Award, a National Award, or a Certificate of Participation.

Unit Essential Question

How do I train, choose one event in each category, and show my best honest effort on the Presidential Fitness Test?

Bell Ringer/Warm-up

Enter and sit on squad spots. Coach reads the makeup list from the official sheets (absent, incomplete, interrupted test, or illness). Already-finished students sit in a helper or quiet-station group. Makeup students warm up in a small group (about 5 minutes) for the event they will complete. Freeze.

Direct Instruction (TW) Teacher

This is still official testing, not a casual practice day and not a "try again until you like the score" day. Makeup is for students who missed a category or did not get a fair official attempt (absence, illness, timer error, unsafe weather, or a similar reason applied the same way for everyone). Students who already have a fair official score keep that score.

Explain stations: Makeup Core, Makeup Cardio, Makeup Upper Body. Students go only to the category they are missing. Same school form rules, same two-miss beep rule, same measured mile, same official recording.

Finished students: they may be silent counters or lane helpers if they can be fair, or they use the quiet fitness station. Their official scores do not change today.

After makeup scores are written, the coach can finish award checks with the Benchmarks page and prepare Presidential, National, and Participation certificates for grades 3-5 who qualify or who completed the test.

Guided Practice (SW) Students

Call makeup students by category so equipment can be set once.

1. Core makeups (curl-ups together on one timer; planks timed individually).

2. Cardio makeups (mile group or beep wave — do not leave a student untimed).

3. Upper-body makeups (push-up official set; pull-ups at the bar with the coach counting).

Record, then check the sheet: every makeup student should now have three scores or a written note (injury, still absent, medical). Do not invent numbers for anyone who could not test.

Cool-down

Whole class walk and stretch (3 min), including helpers and makeup finishers.

Kagan/SLAMified — RallyRobin

With a partner (mixed finished and makeup students after testing is done): take turns naming one safe, fair testing habit (honest counting, trying your best, cheering quietly, following the school form rule). Two short rounds.

Exit Ticket

Coach does a final sheet check. Missing scores are highlighted for a later makeup if needed. If the unit is complete, tell the class when certificates will go home — do not read a ranked list aloud.

Reminders

Compare each completed sheet to the Benchmarks page using age on test day, sex as listed on the official tables, and the one event used in each category.

Presidential = all three categories at or better than the Presidential target (mile at or faster than the Presidential time). National = all three at or better than National, but not all three Presidential. Participation = completed the test (grades 3-5 only).

K-2 is not part of official testing or certificates.

Keep a copy of the class list. Send certificates home after checks are done.

Nevada State Standards

  • 1.5.1 (Grades 3-5) — Utilize locomotor and nonlocomotor movements in physical activities.
  • 1.5.4 (Grades 3-5) — Demonstrate safe practices while participating in physical activities.
  • 2.5.3 (Grades 3-5) — Explain the importance of safety rules and procedures for self and others in physical activities.
  • 3.5.1 (Grades 3-5) — Participate in a variety of moderate to vigorous physical activities.
