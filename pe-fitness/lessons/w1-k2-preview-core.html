Superhero core (preview)

Week 1

K–2 students try a few slow curl-ups and a short “superhero” plank. Just for fun: no scores and no testing.

On this page
  • In class
  • Why it matters
  • Practice at home

What we do in class

  1. Warm-up: Fun movement games.
  2. Try it: A few slow curl-ups and a short plank with a partner cheering them on. Kneeling planks are fine.
  3. Looking ahead: Students hear: “In 3rd grade you’ll get to try the Presidential Fitness Test!”

Why it matters

Playful practice helps younger students feel excited and ready for 3rd grade.

Practice at home

  • Hold a superhero plank and count to ten.
