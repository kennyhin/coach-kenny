Fitness Preview — Superhero Core (Curl-ups and Short Planks)

When: Week 1 Grades K-2

Equipment

  • Gym mats or clean floor spots
  • whistle
  • cones for squad spots
  • music optional
  • water. No score sheets
  • no official charts
  • no clipboards for times or rep counts.

Today we will

Lesson Objective

Students will be able to try a few slow curl-ups and a short plank hold with a partner watching for a straight body, and hear that in 3rd grade they will get to try the Presidential Fitness Test.

Unit Objective

Students will try kid-friendly mini versions of the Presidential Fitness Test events so they can move safely, have fun, and learn that in 3rd grade they will get to try the official test.

Unit Essential Question

How can I try new fitness skills, stay safe, and have fun while I get a preview of the Presidential Fitness Test?

Bell Ringer/Warm-up

Enter using the practiced routine and sit on squad spots. Fun warm-up (about 4-5 minutes): animal walks (bear, crab), jumping jacks, and "freeze" on the attention signal. Smile and keep it light.

Direct Instruction (TW) Teacher

In every K-2 lesson, say this line clearly at least once: "In 3rd grade you'll get to try the Presidential Fitness Test!"

Today is a preview of core strength — the muscles in your tummy and back that help you sit tall and play hard. We will try two mini games: a few curl-ups (like a small sit-up) and a short superhero plank (a straight body, like a board).

Model a slow curl-up: on your back, knees bent, sit up a little, and go back down with care. Model a short plank: straight body, no sagging hips, then come down. Kindergarten students may hold a kneeling plank or a very short regular plank. This is not a test. We will not write down scores or give awards.

Vocabulary: curl-up, plank, straight body, freeze.

Guided Practice (SW) Students

Partners or small groups. Lots of cheering. No counting for a record.

  1. Station 1 — Curl-up tries (6-7 min): together, do a few slow curl-ups (about 3-8, each student's best comfortable number). Partner says "up, down" nicely. Rest and switch. If a student needs a smaller movement, they lift the chest only as far as is comfortable.
  2. Station 2 — Superhero plank (6-7 min): hold a short plank (even 5-10 seconds is a win). Kneeling planks are welcome. Come down, rest, try once more. Switch.

If the class is large, split in half and switch stations on the whistle. Play a quick "plank statue" freeze game at the end if time remains (2-3 seconds, then wiggle).

Cool-down

A curled rest, slow breaths, and a gentle stretch (2-3 min).

Kagan/SLAMified — Stand Up, Hand Up, Pair Up

  1. Round 1: "Show your partner a straight superhero body (standing tall or a tiny plank)." Round 2: "Tell your partner what we get to try in 3rd grade." (Presidential Fitness Test.) Two rounds.

Exit Ticket

Thumbs: "I tried a curl-up and a short plank safely." Ask one student to repeat the 3rd-grade line. Coach notes students who need help getting down to and up from the mat next time.

Reminders

No scoring, no official times, no awards, no participation certificates for K-2. Do not post official benchmark numbers. Age 5 has no official target anyway — do not invent one.

Keep work times short. Cheer effort, not "who held longest."

Safety: slow movements, no pulling on necks, come down from a plank if it is hard.

Nevada State Standards

  • 1.2.1 (K-2) — Demonstrate the basic elements of movement forms.
  • 1.2.4 (K-2) — Demonstrate safe practices while participating in physical activities.
  • 2.2.3 (K-2) — Identify appropriate safety practices in general space for self and others.
