SLAM! Nevada · K–5 PE · Presidential Physical Fitness Test

Lessons

What we learn in PE, class by class, from the first practice to test day.

About these lessons

Grades 3–5 spend about four weeks learning and practicing each event, then take the official test over three PE classes in early February.

Grades K–2 get a fun preview of the same events during the practice weeks. They are not tested or scored.

Tap any lesson to see what we do in class, why it matters, and an easy way to practice at home.

Grades 3–5

Grades 3–5: Practice weeks

About four weeks of learning and practicing each event before the real test.

  1. 1 Meet the test + curl-upsWeek 1 · Class 1 · We learn how the Presidential Fitness Test works and practice the first core event: curl-ups. →
  2. 2 PlankWeek 1 · Class 2 · Students learn the plank, the other choice in the core category. →
  3. 3 One-mile runWeek 2 · Class 1 · Students learn how to pace a one-mile run so they can finish strong. →
  4. 4 20-meter beep testWeek 2 · Class 2 · Students learn the beep test (also called the PACER), the other choice in the cardio category. →
  5. 5 Right-angle push-upsWeek 3 · Class 1 · Students learn right-angle push-ups, one of the two upper-body choices. →
  6. 6 Pull-upsWeek 3 · Class 2 · Students learn pull-ups, the other upper-body choice. →
  7. 7 Mixed stations + choosing eventsWeek 4 · Class 1 · Students try all six events at stations, then pick one favorite in each category. →
  8. 8 Practice test dayWeek 4 · Class 2 · A dress rehearsal: students try their chosen event in each category, just like test day. These are practice results only, not the official test. →
Grades 3–5

Grades 3–5: Test days

The official test, one category per PE class, plus a makeup day for anyone who was absent.

  1. 9 Test day 1: CoreTesting Day 1 · The official test begins. Students do their core event: curl-ups in one minute or a plank hold. →
  2. 10 Test day 2: CardioTesting Day 2 · Students do their cardio event: the one-mile run or the 20-meter beep test. →
  3. 11 Test day 3: Upper bodyTesting Day 3 · Students do their upper-body event: right-angle push-ups or pull-ups. That finishes all three categories. →
  4. 12 Makeup dayTesting Day 4 · A chance for anyone who was absent or had an attempt interrupted to finish their events. →
Grades K–2 · Just for fun

Grades K–2: Fun preview

Younger students try mini versions of the events during the same weeks. No scores, no testing, no awards.

  1. 13 Superhero core (preview)Week 1 · K–2 students try a few slow curl-ups and a short “superhero” plank. Just for fun: no scores and no testing. →
  2. 14 Short runs + beep games (preview)Week 2 · K–2 students do short runs and a start-stop “beep” running game. No times, no scores. →
  3. 15 Strong arms (preview)Week 3 · K–2 students try wall or knee push-ups and a short hang from a bar. No counting for scores. →
  4. 16 Fun stations (preview)Week 4 · K–2 students rotate through fun mini stations that preview every event. No scores and no awards, just trying everything safely. →
