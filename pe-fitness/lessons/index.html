SLAM! Nevada · K–5 PE · Presidential Physical Fitness Test

Lessons

16 ready-to-teach PE lessons. A substitute can run any of them.

Suggested window (not locked to these exact class dates): practice about January 5–29, 2027; official grades 3–5 testing about February 1–12, 2027, plus a makeup week. Backup window: spring 2027 (late March–April) if weather or the PE calendar requires it.

About these lessons

Official numbers: the Benchmarks page. Age 5 has no official target. Do not invent numbers.

Grades 3–5 only test for awards. Use age on test day. A student earns Presidential or National by hitting one target in each of the three categories (not every event). Completers may receive a Certificate of Participation.

Grades K–2 do not test, do not get scored, and do not receive awards or certificates. They get a kid-friendly preview during the practice weeks. The coach says: “In 3rd grade you’ll get to try the Presidential Fitness Test!”

These plans follow the SLAM PE Planbook template (Unit Objective, Essential Question, Lesson Objective, Bell Ringer/Warm-up, Direct Instruction, Guided Practice, Cool-down, Kagan/SLAMified, Exit Ticket, Reminders, Nevada State Standards), plus Title, Grade band, When, and Equipment so a substitute can run the class. They have not been posted to Planbook.
