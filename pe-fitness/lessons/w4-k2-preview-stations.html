K–2 students rotate through fun mini stations that preview every event. No scores and no awards, just trying everything safely.

What we do in class

  1. Stations: Short stations: a few curl-ups, a superhero plank, a short run, a beep game, wall or knee push-ups, and a short hang or animal walks.
  2. Looking ahead: The class says it together: “In 3rd grade you’ll get to try the Presidential Fitness Test!”

Why it matters

Students leave proud of trying every station and excited for 3rd grade.
