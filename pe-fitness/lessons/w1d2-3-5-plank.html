Today we will

Lesson Objective

Students will be able to hold a straight-body plank using the school form cues while a partner times until form breaks, and name plank as the other core choice besides curl-ups, with the coach seeing safe form from at least 80% of pairs.

Unit Objective

Students will demonstrate safe form and honest effort on the six Presidential Fitness Test events and complete official testing in all three categories (core, cardio, and upper body) so they can earn a Presidential Award, a National Award, or a Certificate of Participation.

Unit Essential Question

How do I train, choose one event in each category, and show my best honest effort on the Presidential Fitness Test?

Bell Ringer/Warm-up

Enter and sit on squad spots. Recap: "Last class we learned the three categories and practiced 1-minute curl-ups. Today is the other core choice — the plank." Dynamic warm-up (about 5 minutes): jog, high knees, easy shoulder taps from a kneeling position, and 10-second practice planks. Freeze on the attention signal.

Direct Instruction (TW) Teacher

Review: for the official test, a student picks curl-ups or plank for the core category — both are not required.

Model a strong plank (school rule, because the official PDF does not spell out form): hands or forearms on the mat, body in a straight line from head to heels, legs straight, hips not sagging or piked up, eyes looking down. The score is how many seconds the student holds that shape. Partner starts the time when the hold begins and stops when form breaks (hips drop, hips pike, knees rest, or the student comes down). Use that same school rule for everyone.

Show a kneeling short plank as form practice, then the full plank. Students who need a modification may start from knees during practice only; official testing later should use the same full-plank rule for everyone unless a documented medical plan says otherwise.

Vocabulary: plank, form break, hold, seconds, core.

Official plank targets (seconds, at least) for common 3-5 ages. Look up other ages in the Benchmarks page. Age 5 has no official number.

Boys Presidential / National: age 8 = 95 / 63; 9 = 109 / 63; 10 = 119 / 65; 11 = 119 / 71.

Girls Presidential / National: age 8 = 92 / 60; 9 = 105 / 66; 10 = 105 / 66; 11 = 105 / 67.

Guided Practice (SW) Students

Partners. One holds, one times, then switch. Several pairs go at once.

  1. Round 1 — Shape check (4 min): 15-20 second holds. Partner looks for a straight line and quietly says "hips" if they sag.
  2. Round 2 — Build (6 min): 30-40 second holds, rest, switch, repeat.
  3. Round 3 — Best hold (8 min): Each student gets two official-style tries (hold until form breaks, or until they choose to stop). Partner may record the better practice time. Coach spots groups that need a form reset.

If time remains, a short curl-up review (one 30-second partner set) so both core options stay fresh.

Cool-down

Child's pose and a gentle standing chest opener (2-3 min).

Kagan/SLAMified — RallyRobin

With a partner, back and forth, name form cues for a strong plank (straight body, hips in line, eyes down, breathe). Two rounds, 30-45 seconds each.

Exit Ticket

Show-me: on the signal, get into a 5-second plank, then stand. At line-up, answer: "Which two events can you pick for core?" Coach notes sagging hips or held breath for 1:1 next class.

Reminders

Plank targets are in seconds and are "at least." A 10-year-old boy's Presidential plank is 119 seconds; National is 65. A 10-year-old girl's Presidential plank is 105 seconds; National is 66.

Do not compare students out loud in a way that shames a short hold. Effort and form first during practice.

Safety: wrists, shoulders, and lower back — come down if it hurts. No one should be kicked or climbed on in a plank.

Nevada State Standards

  • 1.5.1 (Grades 3-5) — Utilize locomotor and nonlocomotor movements in physical activities.
  • 1.5.4 (Grades 3-5) — Demonstrate safe practices while participating in physical activities.
  • 2.5.3 (Grades 3-5) — Explain the importance of safety rules and procedures for self and others in physical activities.
  • 3.5.1 (Grades 3-5) — Participate in a variety of moderate to vigorous physical activities.
