Test day 3: Upper body

Testing Day 3

Students do their upper-body event: right-angle push-ups or pull-ups. That finishes all three categories.

On this page
  • In class

What we do in class

  1. Warm-up: Arm and shoulder warm-up.
  2. Official try: Students do their chosen event while a partner or the coach counts good-form reps.
  3. Celebrate: Students finish all three categories and celebrate their hard work.

Why it matters

After this day, Coach Kenny compares each student’s results to the official targets for their age.
