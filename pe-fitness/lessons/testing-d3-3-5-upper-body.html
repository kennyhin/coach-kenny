Official Testing Day 3 (Upper Body)

Today we will

Lesson Objective

Students will be able to complete their official upper-body event (right-angle push-ups or pull-ups), with a recorded official rep count for every student present, so all three categories can be compared to the official charts after class.

Unit Objective

Students will demonstrate safe form and honest effort on the six Presidential Fitness Test events and complete official testing in all three categories (core, cardio, and upper body) so they can earn a Presidential Award, a National Award, or a Certificate of Participation.

Unit Essential Question

How do I train, choose one event in each category, and show my best honest effort on the Presidential Fitness Test?

Bell Ringer/Warm-up

Enter, attendance, and choice check. Dynamic warm-up (about 5 minutes): arm circles, scapular squeezes, 5 practice push-ups or knee push-ups, easy shoulder stretches. No max-effort pull-ups in warm-up. Freeze. Push-up choosers on one side; pull-up choosers behind the bar cone. Students may change this category's choice before the official start, then it is locked.

Direct Instruction (TW) Teacher

Official upper body today. Pick one: right-angle push-ups or pull-ups.

Push-ups: partner watches from the side and counts only good reps (straight body, elbows to about a right angle, full push-up). The official set is one continuous set. If the student rests on the floor, the set ends (same school rule as practice). Coach spot-checks counts.

Pull-ups: one student at a time. Official count = full reps only (chin over the bar, arms straight at the bottom). No kipping. Coach or a trained aide should be the official counter at the bar. Waiting students stay behind the cone.

After this category is recorded, the coach has all three official scores for students who were present all three testing days. Do not announce awards in a way that shames students. Certificates can be prepared after scores are checked against the Benchmarks page.

Official upper-body targets for common 3-5 ages (Presidential / National). Look up other ages in the Benchmarks page.

Boys push-ups: age 8 = 17 / 9; 9 = 18 / 12; 10 = 22 / 14; 11 = 27 / 15. Boys pull-ups: 8 = 5 / 1; 9 = 5 / 2; 10 = 6 / 2; 11 = 6 / 2.

Girls push-ups: age 8 = 17 / 9; 9 = 18 / 12; 10 = 20 / 13; 11 = 20 / 13. Girls pull-ups: 8 = 2 / 1; 9 = 2 / 1; 10 = 3 / 1; 11 = 3 / 1.

Guided Practice (SW) Students

This period is the official upper-body test.

1. Start the bar line (it is slower). While small groups test at the bar, push-up rows can complete official sets with partners. Best practice: coach scans push-up rows during their official set, then returns to the bar.

2. Record reps immediately. One official attempt, plus a second only if the first was interrupted (same fairness rule as Day 1).

3. Finished students sit in a quiet zone or do an easy stretch. They do not re-test to fish for a higher score unless the coach is using the stated interruption rule.

Mark absences as makeup.

Cool-down

Shoulder and chest stretches, easy walk (2-3 min).

Kagan/SLAMified — Stand Up, Hand Up, Pair Up

After testing. Round 1: "Tell your partner one thing you improved during this unit." Round 2: "What does it mean to hit one target in each category?" Keep it short.

Exit Ticket

Coach verifies an upper-body event and score (or makeup mark) for every student present. If a student's three categories are complete, the sheet can be marked "ready to score for awards." Thumbs: "My upper-body score is written down."

Reminders

Score awards only from official tables: Presidential if all three categories meet Presidential; National if all three meet National but not Presidential; Certificate of Participation for 3-5 students who completed the test. Never invent a missing-age target.

Bar safety still applies on test day. Keep official sheets; print certificates after the makeup day if scores may still change.

Nevada State Standards

  • 1.5.1 (Grades 3-5) — Utilize locomotor and nonlocomotor movements in physical activities.
  • 1.5.4 (Grades 3-5) — Demonstrate safe practices while participating in physical activities.
  • 2.5.3 (Grades 3-5) — Explain the importance of safety rules and procedures for self and others in physical activities.
  • 3.5.1 (Grades 3-5) — Participate in a variety of moderate to vigorous physical activities.
