Grades 3–5

Pull-ups

Week 3 · Class 2

Students learn pull-ups, the other upper-body choice.

On this page
  • In class
  • Why it matters
  • Practice at home

What we do in class

  1. Warm-up: Easy arm and shoulder moves.
  2. Learning the pull-up: Hang from the bar, pull up until the chin is over the bar, then lower all the way down with straight arms.
  3. Safe tries: Students take turns at the bar with a partner or the coach counting. While they wait, they practice push-ups or a short bent-arm hang.
  4. Cool-down: Stretching and a share about which upper-body event they like.

Why it matters

Pull-ups are hard for most students at first. Every try builds grip and back strength, and getting even one is a big win.

Practice at home

  • Monkey bars and hanging at the playground are great pull-up practice.
  • Cheer for effort. Many students start with a hang and build from there.
