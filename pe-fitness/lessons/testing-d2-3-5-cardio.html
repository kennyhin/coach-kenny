Test day 2: Cardio

Testing Day 2

Your child does their cardio event: the one-mile run or the 20-meter beep test.

On this page
  • In class
  • Why it matters
  • Try it at home

What your child does in class

  1. Warm-up: Easy jogging and stretches.
  2. Official try: Mile runners run the measured course and get a finish time. Beep-test runners go between the lines while laps are counted.
  3. Cool-down: Walking, water and stretching.

Why it matters

Cardio is the hardest day for many kids. Pacing practice from the earlier weeks really pays off here.

Try it at home

  • Running shoes, weather-appropriate clothes and a water bottle (cardio may be outside).
  • Sleep and breakfast make a big difference on this day.
