Strong arms (preview)

Week 3

K–2 students try wall or knee push-ups and a short hang from a bar. No counting for scores.

On this page
  • In class
  • Why it matters
  • Practice at home

What we do in class

  1. Warm-up: Arm circles and animal walks.
  2. Push-ups: Wall push-ups or knee push-ups with safe hands.
  3. Hang time: A short hang from a safe bar while taking turns. If there is no bar, students do animal walks instead.

Why it matters

These moves build early arm and grip strength in a safe, playful way.

Practice at home

  • Try wall push-ups, or hang from the monkey bars at the park.
