Test day 1: Core

Testing Day 1

The official test begins. Your child does their core event: curl-ups in one minute or a plank hold.

On this page
  • In class
  • Why it matters
  • Try it at home

What your child does in class

  1. Warm-up: The same warm-up they practiced.
  2. Quick review: The coach goes over form one more time so everyone knows exactly what counts.
  3. Official try: Students do their chosen core event while a partner or the coach counts or times.
  4. Cool-down: Stretching. Students who finish early do light, fun activities.

Why it matters

This is one of three test days, one category per PE class, so no one has to do everything in a single day.

Try it at home

  • Send your child in sneakers and comfortable clothes, with a water bottle.
  • Remind them that their best honest effort is what counts.
