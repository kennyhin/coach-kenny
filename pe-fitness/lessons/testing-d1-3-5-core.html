Today we will

Lesson Objective

Students will be able to complete their official core event (curl-ups in 1 minute or a plank hold), with age, sex, event, and score recorded on the class score sheet for every student present.

Unit Objective

Students will demonstrate safe form and honest effort on the six Presidential Fitness Test events and complete official testing in all three categories (core, cardio, and upper body) so they can earn a Presidential Award, a National Award, or a Certificate of Participation.

Unit Essential Question

How do I train, choose one event in each category, and show my best honest effort on the Presidential Fitness Test?

Bell Ringer/Warm-up

Enter, sit on squad spots, and sit quietly while the coach checks attendance and age on the score sheet. Dynamic warm-up (about 5 minutes): easy jog in place, shoulder rolls, 5 slow curl-ups, 10-second practice plank (practice only — official hold comes later). Freeze. Students go to the curl-up side or the plank side based on their choice. They may change their core choice before the official start, then it is locked for this category.

Direct Instruction (TW) Teacher

This is official core testing. One event only: curl-ups or plank. The score from today is the core score used for awards.

Curl-up rule: partner (or adjacent student) counts good reps using the school form rule from Week 1. Whole curl-up group starts on the coach's "Go" and stops at exactly 1 minute. Write the number of good reps.

Plank rule: partner starts the timer when the official hold begins and stops at form break, or the coach times small waves. Write seconds held.

Fair counting: the coach should spot-check. If a count is clearly wrong, the coach makes the correction. Students waiting stay seated and quiet so timers can be heard.

Official core targets for common 3-5 ages (Presidential / National). Look up other ages in the Benchmarks page. Age 5 has no official number.

Boys curl-ups: age 8 = 40 / 31; 9 = 41 / 32; 10 = 45 / 35; 11 = 47 / 37. Boys plank (sec): 8 = 95 / 63; 9 = 109 / 63; 10 = 119 / 65; 11 = 119 / 71.

Girls curl-ups: age 8 = 38 / 29; 9 = 39 / 30; 10 = 40 / 30; 11 = 42 / 32. Girls plank (sec): 8 = 92 / 60; 9 = 105 / 66; 10 = 105 / 66; 11 = 105 / 67.

Guided Practice (SW) Students

This period is the official test, not a new skill lesson.

1. Curl-up wave (or whole group): official 1-minute trial. Partners switch so both are tested (run two 1-minute rounds). Record scores immediately.

2. Plank waves: several students hold at once, each with a partner timer, or the coach times a row. Record seconds immediately. Give one official attempt; a second attempt only if the first was interrupted (timer error, someone bumping the student, or a similar problem) — use that same makeup rule for everyone.

3. Students who finish early sit in a quiet zone, stretch, or walk the edge. They do not start cardio or upper-body official tests today.

If a student is absent or cannot safely test, mark "makeup" on the sheet. Do not invent a score.

Cool-down

Walk and gentle core stretches (2-3 min). Water.

Kagan/SLAMified — Timed Pair Share

After scores are written, short and after the test only. Round 1: "One thing I did well on core was ___." Round 2: "Tomorrow is cardio — I am picking mile or beep because ___." 20-30 seconds each.

Exit Ticket

Coach checks that every present student has a core event name and a score (or a makeup mark) on the official sheet before dismissal. Thumbs: "My core score is written down."

Reminders

Awards are decided only after all three categories are finished. Do not announce award winners today.

Look up each child's age on test day in the Benchmarks page. Do not use a made-up number for a missing age.

Safety: stop for pain or dizziness. Keep official sheets in a secure folder.

Nevada State Standards

  • 1.5.1 (Grades 3-5) — Utilize locomotor and nonlocomotor movements in physical activities.
  • 1.5.4 (Grades 3-5) — Demonstrate safe practices while participating in physical activities.
  • 2.5.3 (Grades 3-5) — Explain the importance of safety rules and procedures for self and others in physical activities.
  • 3.5.1 (Grades 3-5) — Participate in a variety of moderate to vigorous physical activities.
