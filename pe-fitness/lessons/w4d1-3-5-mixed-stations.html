Today we will

Lesson Objective

Students will be able to rotate through all six events at stations, try both options in each category, and write a first choice (one event per category) on a practice card, with the coach checking that every student has a complete card before dismissal.

Unit Objective

Students will demonstrate safe form and honest effort on the six Presidential Fitness Test events and complete official testing in all three categories (core, cardio, and upper body) so they can earn a Presidential Award, a National Award, or a Certificate of Participation.

Unit Essential Question

How do I train, choose one event in each category, and show my best honest effort on the Presidential Fitness Test?

Bell Ringer/Warm-up

Enter and sit on squad spots. Recap the three categories and both event options in each. Dynamic warm-up (about 5 minutes): jog, high knees, 10-second plank, 5 easy curl-ups, 3 kneeling or regular push-ups, arm circles. Freeze. Show the six stations and the rotation direction.

Direct Instruction (TW) Teacher

Today is a practice circuit, not the official test. The goal is to try both options in each category and start choosing. You will still be able to change your mind before each official testing day.

Review the award rule: one event in each category. You do not have to be strong in all six events. Pick the one in each category that gives you the better chance at the official target.

Station expectations: partners count or time; only good reps; freeze on the whistle; rotate on the coach's signal. Pull-up station — one at a time, no dropping unsafely.

Official targets for common 3-5 ages (Presidential / National). Use age on test day. Look up ages not listed in the Benchmarks page. Age 5 has no official number.

Boys

Age 8: curl-ups 40 / 31; plank 95 / 63 sec; mile 8:48 / 11:05; beep 39 / 19 laps; push-ups 17 / 9; pull-ups 5 / 1.

Age 9: curl-ups 41 / 32; plank 109 / 63 sec; mile 8:31 / 10:30; beep 47 / 23 laps; push-ups 18 / 12; pull-ups 5 / 2.

Age 10: curl-ups 45 / 35; plank 119 / 65 sec; mile 7:57 / 9:48; beep 55 / 26 laps; push-ups 22 / 14; pull-ups 6 / 2.

Age 11: curl-ups 47 / 37; plank 119 / 71 sec; mile 7:32 / 9:20; beep 60 / 31 laps; push-ups 27 / 15; pull-ups 6 / 2.

Girls

Age 8: curl-ups 38 / 29; plank 92 / 60 sec; mile 10:02 / 12:30; beep 29 / 15 laps; push-ups 17 / 9; pull-ups 2 / 1.

Age 9: curl-ups 39 / 30; plank 105 / 66 sec; mile 9:30 / 11:52; beep 35 / 18 laps; push-ups 18 / 12; pull-ups 2 / 1.

Age 10: curl-ups 40 / 30; plank 105 / 66 sec; mile 9:19 / 11:22; beep 41 / 21 laps; push-ups 20 / 13; pull-ups 3 / 1.

A student earns an award by hitting one target in each of the three categories, not every event. The mile is the only "at most" (faster) score; all other events are "at least."

Guided Practice (SW) Students

Six stations, or three category stations with both events at each. Partners. Rotate every 4-5 minutes (about 25-30 min total). Suggested stations:

1. Curl-ups — 30-60 second partner practice.

2. Plank — one hold until form breaks (or a cap if the line is long).

3. Mile pacing — if you have the course, a timed practice lap or a short continuous jog while reviewing lap count; if indoors only, talk-test jog loops plus a course-map check so everyone still knows official mile procedures.

4. 20m beep — a short whistle or beep set; partner tallies laps; two-miss school rule.

5. Right-angle push-ups — one counted set.

6. Pull-ups — one or two attempts; waiting students do wall or knee push-ups.

Near the end, students fill the choice card: age (today), core pick, cardio pick, upper-body pick. Coach or an aide checks cards.

Cool-down

Walk the rotation path once, then full-body stretches (3 min).

Kagan/SLAMified — RallyRobin

With a partner, take turns finishing: "I think I will pick ___ for core because ___." Then cardio, then upper body. Two rounds.

Exit Ticket

Hold up the choice card on the way to line. Coach notes blank cards or students who did not get to a station — they go first next class (practice test day).

Reminders

Practice numbers on cards are not official scores. Official testing is one category per PE period after this week.

Keep the same school form rules you taught in Weeks 1-3. Official numbers live in the Benchmarks page — do not invent any.

If the mile cannot fit in a station today, remind the class of the measured lap count (prior SLAM PE plans have used 10 laps for a mile — confirm the course).

Nevada State Standards

  • 1.5.1 (Grades 3-5) — Utilize locomotor and nonlocomotor movements in physical activities.
  • 1.5.4 (Grades 3-5) — Demonstrate safe practices while participating in physical activities.
  • 2.5.3 (Grades 3-5) — Explain the importance of safety rules and procedures for self and others in physical activities.
  • 3.5.1 (Grades 3-5) — Participate in a variety of moderate to vigorous physical activities.
