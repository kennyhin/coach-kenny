Short runs + beep games (preview)

Week 2

K–2 students do short runs and a start-stop “beep” running game. No times, no scores.

On this page
  • In class
  • Why it matters
  • Practice at home

What we do in class

  1. Warm-up: Movement games to get the wiggles out.
  2. Short run: A short run or walk-run loop at their own pace.
  3. Beep game: Run to a line, freeze, and run back on the signal. The lines are much closer together than the real test.

Why it matters

Running games build stamina and listening skills, and they are fun.

Practice at home

  • Play red light, green light or a “run to the tree and back” game.
