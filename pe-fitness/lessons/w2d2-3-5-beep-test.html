Grades 3–5

20-meter beep test

Week 2 · Class 2

Students learn the beep test (also called the PACER), the other choice in the cardio category.

On this page
  • In class
  • Why it matters
  • Practice at home

What we do in class

  1. Warm-up: Easy jogging and practice turning at a line.
  2. How it works: Students run back and forth between two lines 20 meters apart, reaching the line before each beep. The beeps slowly get faster.
  3. Partner practice: One partner runs while the other counts laps. A runner is done after missing the line twice. Then they switch.
  4. Cool-down: Walking, water and stretching, then a chat about whether the mile or the beep test fits them better.

Why it matters

The beep test is a fun way to build endurance and shows how your fitness grows a little at a time.

Practice at home

  • Play a quick game of “run to the line and back” in the yard or park.
  • Which do you like better, the mile or the beep test?
