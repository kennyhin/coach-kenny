Grades 3–5

Meet the test + curl-ups

Week 1 · Class 1

We learn how the Presidential Fitness Test works and practice the first core event: curl-ups.

On this page
  • In class
  • Why it matters
  • Practice at home

What we do in class

  1. Warm-up: Jogging in place, high knees, arm circles and a few easy sit-ups to get moving.
  2. How the test works: There are three categories: core, cardio and upper body. In each one, students pick one of two events. Awards are based on age on test day, not grade.
  3. Learning curl-ups: Knees bent, feet flat, arms crossed on the chest. Sit up, then lower back down until the shoulder blades touch the mat. No pulling on the neck.
  4. Partner practice: One partner works while the other counts good reps, then they switch. Students build up to a full one-minute try. These are practice numbers, not the real test.
  5. Cool-down: An easy walk and gentle stretches, then partners quiz each other on the three categories.

Why it matters

A strong core helps with posture, balance and almost every sport and game.

Practice at home

  • Can you name the three categories (core, cardio, upper body) and the two event choices in each?
  • Try a few slow curl-ups. Good form matters more than speed.
