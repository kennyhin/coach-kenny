Introduction and Curl-ups

Today we will

Lesson Objective

Students will be able to explain that they must hit one target in each of three categories (core, cardio, upper body) and complete a partner-counted 1-minute curl-up practice using the school form cues, with the coach seeing safe form from at least 80% of pairs.

Unit Objective

Students will demonstrate safe form and honest effort on the six Presidential Fitness Test events and complete official testing in all three categories (core, cardio, and upper body) so they can earn a Presidential Award, a National Award, or a Certificate of Participation.

Unit Essential Question

How do I train, choose one event in each category, and show my best honest effort on the Presidential Fitness Test?

Bell Ringer/Warm-up

Students enter using the practiced routine and sit on squad spots. Dynamic warm-up (about 5 minutes): jog in place, high knees, arm circles, easy toe touches, and 10 easy sit-ups at a slow pace. Freeze on the attention signal.

Direct Instruction (TW) Teacher

Explain the unit: grades 3-5 will practice for the official Presidential Fitness Test and then test in late January or early February. Awards are by age on test day, not by grade.

Teach the award rule in plain words: there are three categories. In each category you pick one event and try to hit the target. Core = curl-ups in 1 minute or a plank hold. Cardio = the 1-mile run or the 20-meter beep test. Upper body = right-angle push-ups or pull-ups. Hit the Presidential number in all three categories to earn the Presidential Award. Hit the National number in all three (but not all Presidential) to earn the National Award. Everyone in 3-5 who completes the official test may receive a Certificate of Participation.

Today's event is curl-ups. The official PDF does not spell out every form detail, so use this school rule the same way for every student: lie on your back, knees bent, feet flat, arms crossed on your chest or hands at your shoulders; sit up until your elbows or chest come toward your thighs; lower down until your shoulder blades touch the mat; that counts as one. Partners may gently hold feet if the student wants that. No pulling on the neck.

Introduce vocabulary: category, target, curl-up, Presidential, National, age on test day.

Official curl-up targets (reps in 1 minute, at least) for common 3-5 ages. Look up ages 7 or 12+ in the Benchmarks page. Age 5 has no official number.

Boys Presidential / National: age 8 = 40 / 31; 9 = 41 / 32; 10 = 45 / 35; 11 = 47 / 37.

Girls Presidential / National: age 8 = 38 / 29; 9 = 39 / 30; 10 = 40 / 30; 11 = 42 / 32.

Guided Practice (SW) Students

Partners. One works, one counts, then switch.

  1. Round 1 — Form only (4 min): 8-10 slow curl-ups. Partner watches knees, feet, and a controlled lower-down. Coach walks and fixes form.
  2. Round 2 — Short timed practice (6 min): 30 seconds of curl-ups. Partner counts only good reps. Rest and switch.
  3. Round 3 — Full 1-minute practice (8 min): Coach starts the whole class with "Go" and ends with "Stop." Partner counts. Students may record a practice number, but this is not the official test. Switch so both partners get a full minute. A second 1-minute try is optional if time remains.

Cool-down

Easy walk around the gym, then knee-to-chest and gentle twist stretches on the mat (2-3 min).

Kagan/SLAMified — Stand Up, Hand Up, Pair Up

On signal, students stand, hand up, and pair with the nearest classmate. Round 1: "Tell your partner the three categories and the two event choices in each." Round 2: "Show or tell one curl-up form cue." Rotate partners 2 rounds.

Exit Ticket

Thumbs-up/sideways/down at line-up: "Can you name one core choice (curl-ups or plank) and one form cue for curl-ups?" Coach notes students who cannot name the rule or who pulled on their necks — 1:1 next class.

Reminders

Do not invent numbers. Official charts are in the Benchmarks page.

This is practice, not official testing. Keep the school curl-up rule consistent.

Safety: stop if someone feels dizzy, has neck or back pain, or cannot breathe well. Use mats if the floor is hard.

Nevada State Standards

  • 1.5.1 (Grades 3-5) — Utilize locomotor and nonlocomotor movements in physical activities.
  • 1.5.4 (Grades 3-5) — Demonstrate safe practices while participating in physical activities.
  • 2.5.3 (Grades 3-5) — Explain the importance of safety rules and procedures for self and others in physical activities.
  • 3.5.1 (Grades 3-5) — Participate in a variety of moderate to vigorous physical activities.
