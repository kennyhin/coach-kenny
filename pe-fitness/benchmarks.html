Age on test day

Targets go by age on test day, not grade. (The White House does not define age yet; this is our school rule.)

Age 5 has no official numbers

The official chart starts at age 6. Kindergarteners who are 5 do preview stations only, so their row says “No official number.”

Where the numbers come from

Presidential numbers come from the official PDF ↗ (opens in a new tab) and the White House fitness page ↗ (opens in a new tab). National numbers come from the White House fitness page only, not the PDF.
